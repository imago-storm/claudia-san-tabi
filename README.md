# claudia-san-tabi

A browser tool that draws a printable, full-size **tabi** (Japanese split-toe sock) sewing pattern for your own foot measurements.

Enter the length and width from a tracing of your foot (plus optional circumference, height, instep, heel room and fabric thickness), and it draws the bottom, inpiece and outpiece, then splits them over A4 sheets to print at 100%.

Everything runs in one static file, `index.html`. No build step, no server, no dependencies.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the repository with GitHub Pages (Settings, Pages, deploy from the `main` branch, root folder).

1. Read the **How to measure** section on the page.
2. Enter your measurements. Lengths and circumferences are in cm, small adjustments in mm.
3. Press **Print on A4 sheets**, or **Download printable file** and print that file. Turn off "fit to page" / scaling, then check that the 5 × 5 cm square measures 5 cm.
4. Make a test pair from cheap fabric before you cut the good cloth.

## Source and credit

The shapes, marks and fitting method come from:

> John Marshall, *Make Your Own Japanese Clothes: Patterns and Ideas for Modern Wear*. Tokyo and New York: Kodansha International, 1988. Chapter 16, "Tabi Socks". ISBN 0-87011-865-X.

The outlines of the bottom, inpiece and outpiece (Figs. 16.4 to 16.6) were traced from the book and are scaled using the book's own method (Figs. 16.7 and 16.8). If you like this tool, please buy or borrow the book: it has the cutting layout, sewing order and finishing steps this tool leaves out.

Extras added here and **not** from the book: the circumference and height inputs, the heel room, the allowance for lining and thick fabric, and the mm-based instep adjustment. They are reasonable estimates, so test with a muslin first.

This is an independent project. It is not made, checked or endorsed by the author or the publisher, and any mistakes in the shapes are ours, not the book's.
